# CIndRA Terminology

- **CIndRA**: Climate Indicator Report Assistant.
- **Relative sea level**: Sea level measured by a tide gauge relative to the land at the station.
- **Absolute sea level**: Sea level measured by satellite altimetry in a geocentric reference frame.
- **Product profile**: Either `Regional` or `National/EEZ`. Profiles change spatial scope and presentation, not scientific methods.
- **Storm year**: May 1 through April 30, labeled by its starting year.
- **Flood day**: A day whose maximum water level reaches or exceeds `30 cm above MHHW`.
- **Level 2**: CIndRA's prototype tide-gauge completeness and station-suitability rule.
- **Primary product**: A required report-facing output supported by an approved workflow.
- **Supporting**, **diagnostic**, and **optional product**: Non-primary outputs that must be labeled by role.
- **Draft / Experimental**: Output that has not received the scientific review required for report approval.
